What the high-k tilings look like as a set

They are worth looking at as a set. The high-k tilings are not subtle variations on a theme — some are stripes, some are pinwheels, some are long interlocking chevrons, and the 6-orbit polyrhombilles alone run through most of that range. What they share is that no arrangement with fewer orbits exists, which is exactly the thing the pictures cannot show you.

Ordered by isohedral number, then family. The first is the chevron at ten orbits, the second the 11-hex at nine — the record and the honest record. The three shapes whose tiling structure could not be classified (Part IV) are still drawn here; only their combinatorial type is unknown, not their tiling.

Written for the hand-curated 31-tiling gallery; the autogenerated galleries that replaced it draw all 272 anisohedral tilings at k ≥ 4. A related fact that lived beside it: the 62 club shapes reduce to 49 distinct polygons under boundary congruence — a one-sided 11-cell polyrhombille and a 22-cell polypon trace the same outline, as do a polypon and its one-sided twin at n = 10 (count_club.py --gallery derives this).
